About me

I'm a Statistics and Machine Learning student at Carnegie Mellon University (Class of 2027), originally from Nanjing, China. I'm drawn to problems where measurement, language, and machine learning meet — from designing rubrics that quantify how clearly humans and AI judges communicate, to teaching a robot arm to plan its own motion.

Outside of research and coursework, I've spent time as a peer tutor for Introduction to Machine Learning, and I like hackathons for the same reason I like research: both reward figuring out the actual constraints of a problem before you start building.
